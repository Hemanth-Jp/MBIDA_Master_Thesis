\chapter*{Use of AI Tools}

During the preparation of this thesis, the AI assistant Claude (Anthropic) was used for the following supporting tasks:

\begin{itemize}
  \item creating and refining the flowcharts and diagrams (TikZ figures) that illustrate the pipeline, hierarchy and reconciliation concepts;
  \item writing detailed code comments and explanatory Markdown cells in the Jupyter notebooks and the Streamlit application;
  \item language editing, consistency checks of figures and cross-references, and proofreading of the \LaTeX{} sources of the thesis report, user manual and literature presentation;
  \item drafting the English abstract and translating it into the German Kurzfassung.
\end{itemize}

The research questions, the methodology, the implementation of the forecasting pipeline and the dashboard, the experiments, and the interpretation of the results are my own work. I have reviewed, verified and, where necessary, corrected all AI-assisted output, and I take full responsibility for the content of this thesis.
